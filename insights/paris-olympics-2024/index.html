Get Ready for the Paris 2024 Olympics with BANN & Associates

June 4, 2026 2 min read

The much-anticipated Paris 2024 Olympic and Paralympic Games are approaching, and it's essential to ensure you have the right travel documents before planning your journey to France. Below, we provide crucial information from the French Embassy to help you navigate the visa application process efficiently and successfully.

Key Olympic and Paralympic Dates

  • Olympic Games: 26 July – 11 August 2024
  • Paralympic Games: 28 August – 8 September 2024

Plan your travel accordingly to ensure you arrive with sufficient time to obtain any necessary visa documentation.

Visa Categories & Requirements

Accredited Persons "Olympic Family"

This category includes athletes, media personnel, officials, and other individuals with accreditation applications submitted to the Olympic or Paralympic Committee.

  • Application Process: Applications can be made at any VFS Global Centre without prior appointment
  • Required Documents: OCOG "Certificate" and valid travel document
  • Fees: No visa fee or service fee required

Guests of the OCOG

Individuals invited by the Olympic Committee must follow the ordinary visa application procedure.

  • Application Process: Register via France-Visas portal
  • Required Documents: "Letter of Invitation" from OCOG, valid travel document, and supporting documents from registration receipt
  • Fees: No visa fee or service fee required

Volunteers

Individuals volunteering for the Olympic or Paralympic Games have a streamlined application process.

  • Application Process:Register via France-Visas portal
  • Required Documents:"Letter of Attestation of Voluntary Service" from COJO, valid travel documents, and supporting documents
  • Fees: Visa and service fees apply standard rates

Spectators

General visitors attending the Games must follow the normal visa application procedure for short-stay or transit visas, depending on their circumstances.

Applying for a Visa from Ghana

The Application Process

Visa applications in Ghana must be submitted via the VFS Global website, including a pre-payment of the service fee. The French Embassy in Accra examines all applications and is the sole authority for visa decisions. All applications for metropolitan France are processed by the Accra embassy, while applications for overseas French territories are handled according to the destination territory's requirements.

Types of Short-Stay Visas Accepted

  • Official Missions: For diplomats and government officials
  • Diplomatic and Service Passport Holders: Expedited processing available
  • Transit Visas: For travelers passing through France
  • Tourism Visas: For leisure travel and sporting events
  • Business Visas: For business-related travel
  • Study Visas: For educational purposes

Important Tips for Your Visa Application

Timeline & Document Preparation

  • Short-stay visas: Submit applications at least 15 days before your departure
  • Long-stay visas: Submit applications at least 1 month before your departure
  • Early Submission: Applications can be submitted up to 6 months prior to your travel date
  • Language Requirements: All documents must be in French or English, or accompanied by an official translation

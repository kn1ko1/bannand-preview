Biography

"Commercially sound, solution-oriented legal advice tailored to strategic objectives."

Freda Nsarkoh is an Associate at BANN & Associates, where she advises clients on a broad range of corporate and commercial matters, including corporate governance, regulatory compliance, company secretarial practice, contract drafting and review, and general commercial advisory work. Her Master of Laws in International Corporate Governance, combined with her MBA, enhances her ability to provide commercially focused legal solutions that align legal strategy with broader business objectives.

Prior to joining the firm, Freda gained valuable experience through professional engagements with leading institutions, including AngloGold Ashanti (Ghana) Limited, the Commission on Administrative Justice (Obuasi). Her experience spans corporate advisory, governance and compliance, legal risk assessment, contract management, and stakeholder engagement across diverse industries.

Freda is particularly interested in corporate and commercial law, governance and compliance, cross-border transactions, and the evolving legal and regulatory landscape affecting businesses operating in Ghana and internationally. She is passionate about continuous professional development and is building a practice focused on providing high-quality legal and business advisory services to both local and international clients across multiple sectors.
